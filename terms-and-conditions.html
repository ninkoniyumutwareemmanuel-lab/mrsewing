1. About Mr. Sewing

Mr. Sewing is a legally operating fashion house and bespoke tailoring brand based in Kigali, Rwanda. It operates under NINKONIYUMUTWARE NEXUS LTD (“the Company”, “we”, “us” or “Mr. Sewing”).

We are dedicated to designing and making high-quality, locally made suits for men and women, produced strictly on order.

2. Definitions

  • Customer / you: anyone who contacts us, books an appointment, places an order or pays for our services.
  • Garment: any suit or other item we make or alter for you.
  • Order: your request for a garment that we have quoted and you have accepted.
  • Deposit: the advance payment of at least 70% of the order value.
  • Official start: the moment we send you the message “Your order is officially started.”
  • Express order: an order that you ask us to complete faster than our standard time.
  • Workshop: our main workshop in Karama, Norvège, Kigali.

3. Acceptance & eligibility

By placing an order, paying a deposit or paying for any of our services, you confirm that you have read, understood and agreed to these Terms & Conditions. If you do not agree, please do not place an order.

Payment means agreement: when you pay for our service, it means you have already agreed to our Terms & Conditions.

If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to these terms for you, including the use of photos in section 15.

4. Our services

4.1 Custom suits

  • Our focus: custom-made suits for men and women, tailored to your measurements, style and fabric choices.
  • Made to order: we do not keep ready-made clothes in stock.
  • What we do not sell: standalone trousers, coats or dresses are not part of our regular range.
  • Other requests: if you need something outside our suits, ask us. We may agree to make it on request, at our discretion, and we will quote it separately before starting.

4.2 Your own fabric, sketches and designs

  • What you can bring: your own fabric, and a sketch, drawing or inspiration photo. In a custom design and sketch consultation we go through your design with you and take your measurements.
  • Price: the price depends on your fabric and on everything your design needs, such as lining and other materials. The tailoring itself follows our standard prices, unless you choose an express order (section 8). We tell you the price before you pay the deposit.
  • Fabric you supply: we check your fabric before we agree the job. We are not responsible for faults that are already in fabric you supply.

4.3 VIP fittings (mobile house calls)

  • What it is: our tailor can come to your home, office or hotel in Kigali for your consultation, measurements or fitting. Request it on our booking page.
  • Transport fee: a transport fee of 10,000 RWF around Kigali applies for our tailor to travel to you.
  • Other charges: we confirm the address, the time and any other charge with you before the visit.

4.4 Alterations and repairs of clothes you already own

  • How it works: call us on +250 787 411 501, or bring your clothes to our workshop. We inspect them first.
  • Price: we give you the actual price after the inspection. It depends on the quality of the fabric, the work to be done, how the garment is built, and when you need it finished.
  • Starting prices: women’s dresses from 50,000 RWF, shirts from 50,000 RWF, trousers from 25,000 RWF and suits from 100,000 RWF. These are starting prices, not final prices.
  • Start of work: we start the work once you agree to the price.

  • Ways to book: use our booking page, or call or message us on +250 787 411 501. Options include a consultation, a workshop visit, a VIP mobile house call within Kigali, and a garment adjustment appointment.
  • Confirmation: a booking request is not confirmed until we confirm the date and time with you by WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • Where we meet: our main workshop is at Kigali, Nyarugenge, Karama, Norvège, KN 441 St. The MIC Building, Downtown, is by booking or appointment only. Please call us before visiting so we can give exact directions and prepare.
  • House calls: a transport fee of 10,000 RWF around Kigali applies (see section 4.3). The address, timing and any other charge are confirmed with you before the visit.
  • Hours: Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Saturday and Sunday by booking or call. We are closed on the last Saturday of each month for Umuganda.

6. Quotes, pricing & discounts

  • Published prices apply to our standard fabric tiers. Special or premium fabrics and highly custom designs may cost more.
  • Your quote: before production we confirm your price in writing, for example by WhatsApp. The quote you accept is the price of your order.
  • Own fabric and sketches: priced as explained in section 4.2.
  • Alterations and repairs: starting prices are in section 4.4. The actual price is confirmed after inspection.
  • VIP house calls: the transport fee is 10,000 RWF around Kigali (section 4.3).
  • Discounts: 5% off when you order 2 or more items, or when you are a returning customer. 10% off when you refer a friend who makes a purchase. We apply any discount when we prepare your quote.
  • Payment methods are confirmed with you when your order is quoted.

7. Orders, deposit & payment

  1. We agree your design, fabric and measurements, and you accept our quote.
  2. You pay a deposit of at least 70% of the order value.
  3. Once we confirm your deposit, we send “Your order is officially started.” Your production timeline starts from that message.
  4. You pay the remaining 30% before or at the time of final pickup or delivery.

We do not start production before the deposit is confirmed.

8. Production timelines & express orders

  • Standard production: 1 to 2 weeks from the official start.
  • Express production: 1 to 3 days from the official start, with an additional 35% fee on the total order value. Express orders depend on workshop availability, and we will tell you whether we can accept yours.
  • Alterations and repairs: we agree the time with you at the inspection. It depends on the job and on when you need your clothes back. A shorter deadline can change the price.
  • Our commitment: we work to the ready date we confirm to you. Delays caused by missing information or late payment from the customer, or by events in section 17, may move that date. We will tell you promptly if that happens.

9. Customer responsibilities & fittings

  • Accurate information: give us correct details about your measurements, design, fabric and contact information.
  • Self-measurement: if you send your own measurements remotely, Mr. Sewing is not responsible for fitting errors caused by inaccurate self-measurement.
  • In-person fittings: we recommend professional measurements at our workshop, by appointment. Please attend booked fittings on time.
  • Payments and replies: pay on time and answer our messages so we can keep your order moving.
  • Inspiration photos and sketches: if you share a design, sketch or photo, you confirm you may use it for this purpose.

10. Design, fabric & changes

  • You approve the design, fabric and measurements before production starts.
  • After the official start, changes may not be possible. If we agree to a change, extra cost and time may apply.
  • Fabric colour and texture can look slightly different on screens and in different light. Handcrafted garments may show small natural variations. This is normal and is not a defect.

11. Completion, collection & delivery

  • We notify you when your garment is ready. Your 7-day free storage period starts from that notification.
  • Collect your garment from our workshop, or have it delivered where delivery has been agreed. Delivery details are confirmed with you in advance.
  • If we use a courier or delivery partner, we share only the information needed to complete the delivery.
  • Please check your garment when you receive it and tell us promptly if something is wrong (see section 13).

12. Uncollected garments & storage fees

To manage space in our workshop and keep operations running on time, these rules apply to finished garments waiting for pickup:

  • Free period: 7 calendar days (1 week) from the date you receive our notification that your order is ready.
  • Daily fee: from the 8th calendar day, a storage fee of 10% of the total cost of the product applies for each day the garment stays uncollected. Fees accumulate and must be paid, together with any balance, before the garment is released.
  • Abandoned garments: when accumulated fees reach 50% of the product cost (day 12), the garment is considered abandoned. It is no longer available to the customer, and ownership rights and deposits paid are forfeited.
  • Resale: Mr. Sewing may resell, re-tailor or liquidate abandoned garments to recover storage fees and unpaid production costs.
