Official Store Policies

Terms & Conditions, Storage Rules, Return Policy, and Privacy Policy

Welcome to Mr. Sewing Fashion. We are committed to crafting high-quality, bespoke suits and shirts tailored to your exact measurements and design specifications. To ensure a smooth experience and maintain clear expectations, please carefully read our complete operational policies below.

1. Terms & Conditions

1.1 Scope of Specialization

  • Products Offered: We specialize exclusively in custom-crafted complete suits and shirts.
  • Exclusions: We do not produce or sell separate trousers, separate coats/blazers without a matching set, or dresses.

1.2 Order Commencement & Payment Terms

  • Deposit Requirement: Production on any order begins only after a minimum 70% advance payment (deposit) is confirmed.
  • Official Confirmation: Your order timeline officially starts when you receive our confirmation message stating: "Your order is officially started."
  • Final Balance: The remaining 30% balance must be settled in full prior to or at the time of final pickup/delivery.

1.3 Production Timeline & Rush Orders

  • Standard Production: Standard custom orders take 1 to 2 weeks to craft from the date of official order confirmation.
  • Express Production: Rush orders required within 2 to 3 days are subject to an additional 35% express fee on the total order value. Express orders are subject to workshop availability.

1.4 Fitting & Measurements

  • In-Person Measurement: Clients may visit our workshop located in Karama for professional fittings. Please contact us in advance at +250 787 411 501 to schedule an appointment.
  • Client-Provided Measurements: If you choose to submit your own measurements remotely, Mr. Sewing Fashion is not responsible for fitting errors resulting from inaccurate self-measurement.

2. Uncollected Garments & Storage Fee Policy

To manage space in our workshop and ensure timely operations, the following strict rules apply to completed garments awaiting pickup:

2.1 Free Grace Period

Completed garments are held free of charge for seven (7) calendar days (1 week) starting from the date you receive official notification that your order is ready for pickup or delivery.

2.2 Daily Storage Fee Calculation

  • Fee Structure: Starting from the 8th calendar day, a daily holding and storage fee equivalent to 10% of the total cost of the product per day will be applied for every day the item remains uncollected.
  • Accumulation: Storage fees will accumulate daily and must be paid in full along with any remaining order balance before the garment is released.

2.3 Forfeiture & Resale of Uncollected Garments

  • 50% Threshold: Once accumulated storage fees reach 50% of the total cost of the product (on the 5th day after the grace period ends, or Day 12 total), the item is deemed abandoned.
  • Loss of Claim: When the accumulated storage fees reach or exceed 50% of the product cost, the item is no longer available to the customer, and all ownership rights and deposits paid are forfeited.
  • Resale Right: Mr. Sewing Fashion reserves the absolute right to resell, re-tailor, or liquidate abandoned garments to recover lost workshop storage fees and unpaid production costs.

3. Refund & Return Policy

3.1 Custom-Crafted Items

Because every garment is bespoke—handcrafted specifically to your unique measurements, style selections, and fabric choices—we do not offer cash refunds, order cancellations, or returns once production has officially started.

3.2 Free Adjustments & Quality Guarantee

We are committed to delivering a perfect fit. If you experience any fitting issues or discover manufacturing defects upon receiving your garment:

  • Notification Window: You must notify us within 72 hours (3 days) of receiving or picking up your item.
  • Correction Service: We will provide free adjustments and alterations to rectify fitting issues or defects within this 72-hour window, provided the garment has not been worn, altered by third parties, or damaged.
  • Late Notifications: Adjustment requests made after the 72-hour window will incur standard alteration fees.

4. Privacy Policy

At Mr. Sewing Fashion, we respect your personal data and privacy.

4.1 Data Collection & Usage

We collect essential personal information—including your full name, phone number, physical measurements, delivery address, and style preferences—solely for the purpose of fulfilling your custom order, managing billing, and delivering personalized customer service.

4.2 Data Protection & Third-Party Sharing

  • Confidentiality: Your personal measurements and contact details are kept secure and strictly confidential within our management system.
  • Third Parties: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal details to third parties. Information is only shared with trusted third-party courier/delivery services strictly necessary to fulfill order deliveries.

5. Contact Information

For fittings, inquiries, or policy questions, please contact us at:

  • Phone / WhatsApp: +250 787 411 501
  • Location: Karama Workshop
