About Mr Sewing

Mr Sewing Fashion Designer operates under NINKONIYUMUTWARE NEXUS LTD in Kigali, Rwanda.

Orders and payments

Pay a 70% advance before production. For a suit order, the balance is due when tailoring is complete, before final cleaning and delivery. Full payment is required before garment release.

Standalone alterations and cleaning require a 70% advance. With that advance, the remaining 30% is payable when the service is complete, before release or delivery. For standalone cleaning, this means after cleaning is complete.

With a 70% advance, the balance is 30%. Your actual balance is the agreed total less payments already received.

Agree the design, fabric, measurements, price and arrangements before production. Production does not begin before the advance is confirmed. Your production start and schedule are confirmed directly with the team.

Suit contents and included package

A standard two-piece suit includes a jacket and trousers. A standard three-piece includes a jacket, trousers and waistcoat. A shirt is not automatically included. Skirt-suit options require a separate quotation and confirmation; the standard trouser-package price must not be assumed.

The standard suit package includes the agreed suit and standard tailoring, a branded hanger and protective cover, one initial standard professional cleaning, and initial door-to-door delivery within Kigali. These included items are not charged separately again.

Appointments and enquiries

A website submission or WhatsApp message is a request, not a confirmed appointment or accepted production order. Wait for direct confirmation of the date, time, location and scope before relying on arrangements.

If you cannot attend, contact the team as early as possible to discuss rescheduling.

Mobile fitting visits are separately arranged. Confirm the location, availability and any charges before a visit.

Planning production

Standard tailoring usually takes 2–4 days; express tailoring usually takes 12–15 hours. These are tailoring-stage estimates, not delivered-suit deadlines. Sourcing, scheduling, fittings, changes, final cleaning and delivery affect the full order timeline. Confirm a realistic completion date individually.

Alterations are usually completed within one maker day. This is a work-stage estimate, not a guaranteed handover time; confirm assessment, scheduling, fitting and delivery arrangements individually.

Confirm the cleaning service, suitability and estimated completion date with the team before proceeding. The full schedule depends on the garment, treatment and current capacity.

Changes to an order

Contact the team before requesting a change to design, fabric, colour or measurements. Ask whether the change is feasible and how it would affect the quotation, work and schedule before deciding how to proceed.

Measurements and fittings

Share your design references, garment needs and measurement information. Discuss fit preferences and the fitting appointments needed for your order.

If you believe a garment does not match the agreed order or has a fit problem, contact us promptly with the order details, a description and photographs if useful. Arrange an assessment with the team. Describe any measurements supplied remotely, wear, damage or previous alterations so the concern can be assessed.

Completion, collection and delivery

Initial door-to-door Kigali delivery is included with the standard suit package. Outside-Kigali courier or agency charges are customer-paid and confirmed before dispatch. A courier deadline must be confirmed individually. Mobile fittings, collection visits and follow-up transport are separate arrangements; confirm their scope and any charges directly.

Full payment is required before release. Wait for readiness confirmation, agree handover arrangements and inspect the garment on receipt. Contact the team if you cannot receive or collect it as arranged.

Cancellation, refunds and concerns

If you want to cancel before production has officially started, contact us as soon as possible with your order details and reason. The team reviews the order status and any costs already incurred, and explains the proposed outcome. A refund or deduction is considered case by case; neither an automatic refund nor automatic advance forfeiture is promised.

For a request after production starts, contact the team with your order details and concern. We review the circumstances and discuss the available next steps. A request does not itself confirm a cancellation, refund or deduction.

If Mr Sewing cannot complete an order for a reason on our side, we will discuss a solution, which may include a refund for work not delivered.
