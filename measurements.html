Custom Measurement Guide

Follow our easy 12-point step-by-step guide for a flawless bespoke suit fit.

Accuracy Matters

We don't need extra room added — simply measure your body directly. Our expert tailoring team calculates all necessary wearing ease and adjustments for you. Grab a measuring tape and ask a friend to help for the best accuracy!

1

Neck

Measure around your neck at the height where your collar, if buttoned, would naturally sit.

2

Shoulder

Measure straight across the back from the end of the right shoulder seam to the end of the left shoulder.

3

Sleeve Length

Measure from the top of your shoulder down along the arm to the knuckle of your thumb.

4

Bicep

Measure around the fullest part of your bicep with your arm relaxed.

5

Wrist

Measure snugly around the fullest part of your wrist.

6

Chest

Measure around the fullest part of your chest. Ensure the tape is level and firm around the body.

7

Belly

Measure around the widest part of your stomach, typically right across or just above the belly button area.

8

Waist

Measure around your natural waistline at the exact level where you normally wear your trousers' belt.

9

Hip

Measure around your hips and the largest part of your buttocks.

10

Jacket Length

Measure vertically from the base of the back of your neck down to the point where your thumb knuckle rests.

11

Trousers Length

Measure from the top of your waistband down your outer leg to your desired ankle length.

12

Thigh

Measure around your upper thigh at its widest point just below the crotch.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I measure myself?

Yes, our guide makes it easy to measure at home. You only need a measuring tape, though asking a friend to help ensures maximum accuracy.

Should I add extra inches to my numbers?

No. Please provide your actual body measurements. Our master tailors will calculate the precise wearing ease based on your preferred suit fit.

Can I request custom adjustments?

Yes! If you have specific fitting preferences (e.g., slim fit vs. classic fit), you can include notes when placing your order.

Can I get my measurements from another tailor?

Yes, if you don't have a tape measure or aren't comfortable taking measurements yourself, you can visit a local tailor to have them measure you based on our guide, and then submit those numbers to us.
