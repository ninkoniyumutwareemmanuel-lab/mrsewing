Our Professional Fitting Protocols

To achieve the flawless silhouette expected of an elite bespoke garment, we recommend the following measurement hierarchy:

Gold Standard

Atelier or Mobile Fitting

Visit our master tailors at the MIC Building / Norvège or schedule a VIP workplace/house call for precision in-person measurement.

Alternative 1

Professional Tailor

Have an experienced local tailor measure you using our 12-point guide if you cannot visit our atelier directly.

Alternative 2

Assisted DIY Method

If taking measurements at home, never do it alone. Always enlist a trusted family member or friend to hold the tape.

Alternative 3

Standard Sizing Reference

Provide your tried-and-true standard suit size (e.g., EU/US sizes or S/M/L/XL) from an unadjusted garment that fits you perfectly.

1

Neck

Measure around your neck at the height where your collar, if buttoned, would naturally sit.

2

Shoulder

Measure straight across the back from the end of the right shoulder seam to the end of the left shoulder.

3

Sleeve Length

Measure from the top of your shoulder down along the arm to the knuckle of your thumb.

4

Bicep

Measure around the fullest part of your bicep with your arm relaxed.

5

Wrist

Measure snugly around the fullest part of your wrist.

6

Chest

Measure around the fullest part of your chest. Ensure the tape is level and firm around the body.

7

Belly

Measure around the widest part of your stomach, typically right across or just above the belly button area.

8

Waist

Measure around your natural waistline at the exact level where you normally wear your trousers' belt.

9

Hip

Measure around your hips and the largest part of your buttocks.

10

Jacket Length

Measure vertically from the base of the back of your neck down to the point where your thumb knuckle rests.

11

Trousers Length

Measure from the top of your waistband down your outer leg to your desired ankle length.

12

Thigh

Measure around your upper thigh at its widest point just below the crotch.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I add extra wearing ease to my measurements?

No. Please provide your exact anatomical body measurements. Our master tailoring team calculates all necessary structural ease and posture adjustments for you.

What if I prefer using my standard suit size?

If you already wear a standard ready-to-wear suit size (e.g., EU/US 40, 42, 44 or S/M/L/XL) from unadjusted garments, you can simply share that size with us during your consultation.

Can I have another professional tailor measure me?

Yes. If you cannot visit our Kigali atelier, you may have an experienced local tailor take your measurements using this 12-point guide and submit the numbers to us.

Can I take my measurements at home?

Yes, but we strictly recommend having a trusted family member or friend assist you to ensure absolute tape accuracy.
