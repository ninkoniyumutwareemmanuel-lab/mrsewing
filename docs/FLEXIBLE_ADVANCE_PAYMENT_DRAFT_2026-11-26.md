## Proposed concept, for review only

A customer might request a below70% initial amount under an individually accepted arrangement, followed by payment of the **entire remaining order price**, not merely a top-up to70%, within an agreed three-day deadline. Missed payment might place the order on hold and require reassessment of completion arrangements. Communicated difficulties might be considered for an agreed extension. Unresolved nonpayment after a week might prompt a human cancellation review. Restarting may require a new agreed quotation, scope and completion date.

These ideas are not automatic rights, fee calculations, funding approval, penalties or customer contractual terms. No percentage from the owner's illustrative20/30/40/50/60% examples is an approved minimum.
