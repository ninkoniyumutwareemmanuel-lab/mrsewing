About Us

Mr. Sewing is a Rwanda-based fashion house and bespoke tailoring brand dedicated to designing, producing, and retailing high-quality locally made apparel for men and women. The brand was founded under NINKONIYUMUTWARE NEXUS LTD.

Our Mission

To empower individuals by providing exceptional, accessible, locally manufactured clothing that fosters national pride, while championing the “Made in Rwanda” initiative with stylish, durable, culturally inspired garments.

Our Vision

To become Rwanda’s leading tailoring and fashion destination, making “Made in Rwanda” apparel the top choice for every citizen.

Why Made to Measure?

Measured to you and drafted to you.

Investing in made-to-measure clothing ensures that every garment is crafted to your precise specifications, offering a flawless silhouette that off-the-rack items simply cannot replicate. Each piece is engineered with careful attention to your individual posture and proportions, giving you complete control over fabrics, linings, and personalized details.

Frequently Asked Questions

Location & Visiting

Where are you located?

Our main workshop is in Kigali, Nyarugenge, Karama, Norvege, KN 441 St. We also operate at the MIC Building Downtown by booking or appointment only.

Do I need to call before visiting?

Yes, please call +250 787 411 501 before visiting so we can provide exact directions and prepare for your consultation.

Ordering & Production

Do you have ready-made clothes in stock?

No. Every garment is custom-made strictly on order to ensure a precision fit.

How long does it take to make a suit?

Standard orders take 1 to 2 weeks. Express orders take 1 to 3 days and require an additional 35% express rush fee.

When does production officially start?

Production begins after a 70% advance deposit is confirmed and you receive our official “Your order is officially started” notification.
