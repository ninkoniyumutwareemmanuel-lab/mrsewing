Our Story

From a Small Workshop
to a Bigger Vision

Mr Sewing's story began with a passion for fashion inspired by family. Introduced to fashion through his father, the founder developed a personal appreciation for clothing, design, and craftsmanship.

In 2019, that passion took shape in a small workshop, where the first Mr Sewing garments were personally crafted for customers.

Behind the workshop was a bigger ambition. For important celebrations, professional occasions, and personal milestones, some customers in Rwanda looked abroad for clothing that reflected their individual preferences.

Mr Sewing was established around a simple belief: personalized clothing, thoughtfully designed and carefully crafted, should also be created here in Rwanda.

Today, the founder primarily manages the business while remaining involved in its creative and tailoring work. The brand continues to put individual expression and attentive service at the heart of what it does.

Our approach

Designed Around You

Every garment begins with a conversation. We take time to understand the occasion, the design you have in mind, and the details that matter to you.

Bring an inspiration photograph or describe your preferred outfit. Together, we discuss fabric and styling options, take or confirm your measurements, and plan the details of your garment.
