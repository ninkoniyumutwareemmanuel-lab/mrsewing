Our Story

Our Work Begins
with Your Needs

Mr Sewing Fashion is a Kigali-based fashion brand operated by NINKONIYUMUTWARE NEXUS LTD.

Our work is centred on the customer: understanding the garment, the occasion and the practical needs behind each request. From custom tailoring to the care of existing clothing, we focus on personal service, fit and craftsmanship.

We bring a structured approach to each service, from the initial consultation and assessment to the agreed quotation, work and handover.

What guides our work

Our Mission

To create exceptional, personalized clothing that combines luxury, quality craftsmanship, and individual expression. Through professional tailoring, thoughtful design, and options for different budgets, we aim to make refined fashion accessible while celebrating the excellence of Made in Rwanda craftsmanship.

What we aspire to

Our Vision

To become one of Rwanda's most trusted and respected fashion houses, recognized for exceptional craftsmanship, personalized luxury, and innovation—demonstrating that world-class fashion can be designed and created right here in Rwanda.

Our approach

Designed Around You

Every service begins with a conversation. We take time to understand your requirements, assess the garment or proposed work and explain the options available.

For custom tailoring, bring an inspiration photograph or describe your preferred outfit. Together, we discuss fabric and styling options, take or confirm measurements and plan the garment. For existing clothing, we assess its condition and the requested alteration, repair or care.

Before work begins, we confirm the scope, applicable quotation and service arrangements. Throughout the process, we focus on professional care, clear communication and the agreed result.

Our commitment

Professional Care.
Clear Communication.

We are committed to understanding your needs, explaining the agreed service and treating each garment with professional care. We aim to deliver work consistent with the confirmed specifications and to assess concerns fairly.
