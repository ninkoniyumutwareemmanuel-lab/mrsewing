Know your fabric

Fabric characteristics

Compare general textile characteristics. Exact fibre percentages, weight, origin and care instructions for your selected cloth are confirmed with the team.

Classic Linen

Linen is made from flax; a classic linen cloth often has a naturally irregular texture.

Texture & feel
Natural-looking, crisp and textured
Climate suitability
Lightweight linen is often suitable for warm conditions.
Best suited for
Shirts, Kaunda suits and relaxed jackets
Considerations & care
Natural linen creases; confirm composition and care. Follow the finished garment’s care instructions.

Textured Linen

A linen-style fabric with more visible irregular or mixed-tone threads.

Texture & feel
Crosshatched or slub-like appearance
Climate suitability
Depends on weight and blend.
Best suited for
Casual jackets, shirts and traditional clothing
Considerations & care
A linen look alone does not establish flax content. Follow the finished garment’s care instructions.

Linen-Cotton Blend

A fabric combining linen and cotton when supplier composition confirms it.

Texture & feel
Often softer with natural texture
Climate suitability
Light constructions can suit warm climates.
Best suited for
Relaxed suits and shirts
Considerations & care
Confirm blend ratio and shrinkage. Follow the finished garment’s care instructions.

Striped Linen

A linen or linen-style fabric with woven or printed stripes.

Texture & feel
Visible stripes with linen-like texture
Climate suitability
Depends on construction and composition.
Best suited for
Shirts and relaxed tailoring
Considerations & care
Confirm pattern, material and care. Follow the finished garment’s care instructions.
