About Mr. Sewing

Bespoke tailoring and custom suits in Kigali, Rwanda, designed around quality, precise fit, and clothing that reflects your personal style.

Who We Are

Mr. Sewing is a Rwanda-based fashion house and bespoke tailoring brand dedicated to designing, producing, and retailing high-quality locally made apparel for men and women, founded under NINKONIYUMUTWARE NEXUS LTD.

Our services include custom suits for men and women, custom shirts, and expert garment alterations, all made strictly on order to ensure a precision fit.

We believe clothing should do more than cover the body. It should help people express their identity, feel confident, and present themselves professionally on every occasion.

Through Mr. Sewing, our goal is to contribute to the growth of quality fashion made in Rwanda.

✦

Our Mission

To empower individuals by providing exceptional, accessible, locally manufactured clothing that fosters national pride, while championing the "Made in Rwanda" initiative with stylish, durable, culturally inspired garments.

◎

Our Vision

To become Rwanda's leading tailoring and fashion destination, making "Made in Rwanda" apparel the top choice for every citizen.

Who We Serve

Mr. Sewing serves individuals who appreciate quality clothing, personal presentation, and thoughtful design. Our intended customers include professionals, business owners, wedding clients, event attendees, and anyone looking for personalized tailoring.

Based in Kigali, Rwanda, we aim to serve customers who want clothing that reflects their needs, preferences, and personal identity.

Why Choose Mr. Sewing?

We aim to make the tailoring experience clear, personal, and focused on the customer's needs.

  • Personalized designs and clothing options
  • Attention to measurements and fit
  • A selection of fabrics and finishing details
  • Standard and express order options, subject to confirmation
  • Communication throughout the order process
  • A focus on quality fashion made in Rwanda
