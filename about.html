About Mr. Sewing Fashion

Bespoke Tailoring & Custom Suits in Kigali, Rwanda

1. What is Mr. Sewing Fashion

Mr. Sewing Fashion is a Rwanda-based fashion house and tailoring brand dedicated to designing, producing, and retailing high-quality locally made apparel for both men and women, founded by NINKONIYUMUTWARE NEXUS LTD.

2. Mission & Vision

Mission:

  • To empower Rwandans from all walks of life by providing exceptional, affordable, and locally manufactured clothing that fosters national pride and confidence.
  • To promote the “Made in Rwanda” initiative by offering stylish, durable, and culturally inspired clothing that competes with international brands in both quality and design.

Vision: To become the leading fashion destination in Rwanda where “Made in Rwanda” clothing becomes the first choice for every citizen, reducing reliance on imported second-hand (which is prohibited) or luxury foreign brands.

3. Products & Services

Mr. Sewing Fashion offers a wide range of fashion solutions tailored to your unique lifestyle:

Bespoke Tailoring

Custom-made outfits for men and women tailored precisely to individual measurements and personal style.

Ready-to-Wear Collections

A refined line of pre-designed clothing available for immediate purchase and instant elegance.

Official & Occasional Wear

Sophisticated designs perfectly suited for weddings, corporate events, and everyday professional wear.

4. Target Market

Mr. Sewing Fashion proudly serves a broad range of discerning customers:

  • Modern Professionals: Individuals seeking stylish, unique, and prestigious designs for the workplace.
  • Budget-Conscious Customers: People looking for premium quality clothing at fair, accessible prices.
  • Supporters of Made in Rwanda: Customers passionate about promoting local products and driving the national economy forward.

5. Competitive Advantage

Exclusive Designs

Unique styles and patterns meticulously crafted so you stand out, not commonly available in the local open market.

Nationwide Delivery

Reliable and secure delivery services across Rwanda for your absolute convenience.

Strong Value Proposition

Uncompromised high-end craftsmanship offered at fair and transparent prices.
