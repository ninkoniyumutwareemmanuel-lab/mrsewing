Cancellations, Refunds & Order Changes FAQs

Find the review process for cancellations, refund requests and proposed changes.

Can I cancel before production starts?

If you want to cancel before production has officially started, contact us as soon as possible with your order details and reason. The team reviews the order status and any costs already incurred, and explains the proposed outcome. A refund or deduction is considered case by case; neither an automatic refund nor automatic advance forfeiture is promised. See the Refund & Cancellation Policy.
Link to this answer : Can I cancel before production starts?

What about a cancellation or refund after work starts?

For a request after production starts, contact the team with your order details and concern. We review the circumstances and discuss the available next steps. A request does not itself confirm a cancellation, refund or deduction. If we cannot complete an order for a reason on our side, we discuss a solution that may include a refund for work not delivered. Nothing on this page limits rights or remedies that apply under law.
Link to this answer : What about a cancellation or refund after work starts?

How do I ask for a refund review?

Send your order details, reason and relevant payment or garment evidence. The team reviews the request and explains the proposed outcome. If a refund is agreed, confirm the amount, method and expected processing time directly.
Link to this answer : How do I ask for a refund review?

Can I change an order after it starts?

Contact the team before requesting a change to design, fabric, colour or measurements. Ask whether the change is feasible and how it would affect the quotation, work and schedule before deciding how to proceed.
Link to this answer : Can I change an order after it starts?

Where can I read the current policy information?

Read the Terms & Conditions and Refund & Cancellation Policy alongside your quotation and order confirmation. Contact the team about your particular circumstances; FAQs do not replace those documents.
Link to this answer : Where can I read the current policy information?
