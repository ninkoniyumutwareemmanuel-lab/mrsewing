Who to contact

Mr Sewing Fashion Designer operates under NINKONIYUMUTWARE NEXUS LTD in Kigali, Rwanda. Contact us with questions about information provided to the business.

Contact Mr Sewing on WhatsApp or phone +250 787 411 501, or email mrsewingfashiondesigner@gmail.com. Include your order or request details so the team can respond in context.

Information you provide

Website forms ask for your name and phone number, an optional email address, and details relevant to the request. Booking forms include preferred dates and times; mobile visit requests include a location.

The measurement form accepts a standard suit-size reference or body measurements, garment and service preferences, an optional delivery location and notes. A linked design enquiry may include its design reference and photograph link.

When you contact the team directly, you may provide design references, garment photographs, measurement details, quotations and payment references. Do not send a card PIN or mobile-money PIN.

Website forms and direct contact

Website booking and measurement forms are submitted through Web3Forms to forward the request to Mr Sewing. They are not sent directly from your browser to a Mr Sewing-owned form server.

Opening a WhatsApp or email link uses the service you select. Information you send there is handled through that service as well as by the business.

Submitting a request does not confirm an appointment or accepted production order.

How request information is used

The team uses the information you provide to respond to your enquiry, discuss designs and measurements, prepare a quotation, coordinate appointments and services, and communicate about payments and handover.

Ask the team about information held for your order or repeat-service needs.

Services involved in processing

Web3Forms processes information entered in website forms so it can be forwarded by email. Email and WhatsApp are also involved when you use those channels. Website hosting and network services process requests needed to load the site.

Where delivery is arranged, relevant recipient and address details are needed for that arrangement. Confirm the delivery provider with the team.

These services have their own privacy information. Review the privacy information of the service you use and contact us with questions about your request.

Photographs and marketing

Placing an order, paying or requesting a service does not by itself authorize advertising use of your photograph. Discuss any proposed photograph or marketing use separately with the team.

Contact us about permission, an existing photograph or a removal request. Include the page or image link so we can identify the photograph.

Website links and technical information

Loading the website involves technical requests to hosting and network services. External links, including Google Maps, WhatsApp and social media, take you to services with their own privacy practices.

Website forms send the fields you complete through Web3Forms. You can instead use the listed phone, WhatsApp or email contact options.

Questions about your records

Contact us to ask about information held by the business, a correction, deletion or a photograph permission. Describe your request and the relevant booking or order. Do not send unnecessary sensitive documents.

Ask the team about retention, providers or other processing details relevant to your request.

Contact

Contact Mr Sewing on WhatsApp or phone +250 787 411 501, or email mrsewingfashiondesigner@gmail.com. Include your order or request details so the team can respond in context.
