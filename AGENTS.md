# Mr Sewing Fashion — mandatory customer-content workflow

Before building, rewriting or connecting any customer-facing page, read `.github/customer-content-reference/README.md`, its current customer-information master, policy source matrix, unresolved register and relevant page-map sections. Use the current pointer, not a remembered older snapshot. This requirement applies to every future Codex task and to shared navigation, forms, SEO and customer communication drafts.

Before writing, inspect the latest accessible approved company Sources and owner decisions and prepare the required `PAGE_BRIEF_TEMPLATE.md` checklist. Identify applicable service description, eligibility, independent service/design/priority journey, quotation/payment, delivery/collection, communication standards, policies, benefits and verified contacts. Cite exact sources/dates/status and rule IDs. Existing website text is comparison evidence, not independent approval. Do not claim an inaccessible source was read.

Use already approved information without asking the owner to repeat it. Latest explicit owner approval takes precedence, followed by latest applicable approved dated policy/addendum, then earlier unsuperseded approval. A newer proposal does not supersede approval. Record absent, conflicting, proposed or conditional information precisely in the unresolved register; continue safe independent work without invented rules.

When new owner-approved decisions arrive, create a dated reference version and update source matrix, unresolved states, current pointer, manifest and changelog together. Preserve previous dated versions and supersession evidence in Git. The reference is not exhaustive while originals are missing. Never copy confidential company finances, private management correspondence, credentials or customer records into this reference or public pages.

Exactly four primary services: Custom Tailoring & Suits; Clothing Alterations & Repairs; Garment Care & Cleaning; Mobile Tailoring, Styling & Event Assistance. Keep Service Experience, Design Order and Priority distinct and preserve context.

Work only on `phase1/customer-journey-repairs` for staging. Never modify/reset/merge into main, deploy production or change live settings. Protect approved homepage text, assets, layout and interactions; identify impact and obtain owner authorisation before affecting it. Customer-facing policy changes require owner review before publication. No final VIP-specific terms/FAQs, membership payments, unverified uploads, invented prices/guarantees or automatic customer messages.

Verify actual page routes, buttons and integrations; do not fake success. Run appropriate build/regression checks and report PASS/FAIL/NOT TESTED/NEEDS OWNER DECISION accurately. Repository instructions make this reference reusable; they do not automatically retrieve unavailable external Sources or establish legal compliance.
