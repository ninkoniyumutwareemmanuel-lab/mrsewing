Alterations & Refinements

Garment adjustments assessed around the fit and changes you need.

A considered refinement.

Customers seeking adjustments to an existing suit, jacket, trousers, dress or skirt.

Measurement illustration; not a customer appointment photograph.

Refine the fit you have

Request an assessment of a suit, jacket, trousers, dress or skirt. The garment’s condition and construction determine which adjustments are possible.

Changes to discuss

Existing booking options include resizing and tapering, sleeve or hem adjustments, zipper or button repairs and lining work. Each request is assessed; not every change is suitable for every garment.

Assessment before a quote

Describe the fit concern and arrange an assessment. The team confirms the work, price, fitting needs and estimated timing before you agree.

Payment and planning

Standalone alterations and cleaning require a 70% advance. With that advance, the remaining 30% is payable when the service is complete, before release or delivery. For standalone cleaning, this means after cleaning is complete. With a 70% advance, the balance is 30%. Your actual balance is the agreed total less payments already received.

Alterations are usually completed within one maker day. This is a work-stage estimate, not a guaranteed handover time; confirm assessment, scheduling, fitting and delivery arrangements individually.

What to share with us

  • Garment type and current fit concern
  • Photographs or a clear description of the requested change
  • Any repairs or previous alterations
  • Preferred timing for assessment

How to begin

  1. Describe the garment and the changes you would like.
  2. Arrange an assessment of fit, condition and construction.
  3. Confirm the work, quote and fitting arrangements.
