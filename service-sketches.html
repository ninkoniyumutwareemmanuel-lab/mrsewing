What to Bring to Your Consultation

  • Inspiration & References: Reference photos, magazine clippings, or personal sketches of the look you desire.
  • Intended Occasion: Details regarding the event (wedding, executive gala, red carpet, or formal ceremony).
  • Fabric & Detail Preferences: Ideas on suiting materials, color tones, linings, or traditional cultural fusion touches.
  • Consultation Booking: Schedule your session via WhatsApp to meet our master designers at our Kigali workspace.
