NINKONIYUMUTWARE NEXUS LTD Kigali, Rwanda Official Store Policy

Our Commitment to Quality, Craftsmanship & Fair Service

At Mr. Sewing Fashion, we create custom-made garments based on each customer's measurements, design preferences, fabric selection, and agreed specifications. Every order represents individual craftsmanship, dedicated production time, and materials selected for that particular customer.

We are committed to providing professional service, clear communication, and a responsible process for addressing genuine fitting concerns and manufacturing defects. This policy explains what customers can expect when requesting a cancellation, refund, adjustment, or other resolution.

Please review this policy before confirming an order or making an advance payment. It should be read together with our Terms & Conditions.

Important Policy Summary

Production begins only after the required advance payment has been confirmed and the customer receives the official message: "Your order is officially started." Once production has officially started, cancellations, returns, and cash refunds are not offered for change of mind on custom-made orders, subject to applicable rights under Rwandan law. Genuine fitting concerns or manufacturing defects should be reported within 72 hours of receiving the garment to be considered for free adjustments.

1. Scope of This Policy

This policy applies to bespoke garments ordered from Mr. Sewing Fashion, operating under NINKONIYUMUTWARE NEXUS LTD, including eligible custom-made suits and shirts.

It covers order cancellations, advance payments, fitting adjustments, reported manufacturing defects, and requests for refunds or other resolutions.

Because our garments are made according to individual customer requirements, they are not treated in the same way as standard ready-made stock. Each request will be assessed according to the confirmed order specifications, the circumstances of the concern, and applicable law.

2. Custom-Made Orders & No-Refund Rule

2.1 Bespoke Production

Custom-made garments are produced for a particular customer using the agreed measurements, design, fabric, and order specifications. Production may involve fabric preparation, cutting, sewing, fitting, and other work that cannot always be reversed or reused for another customer.

2.2 Cancellations After Production Starts

Once the customer has received the official production-start confirmation, the order is considered to be in production. We therefore do not accept ordinary change-of-mind cancellations or provide cash refunds solely because a customer changes their preference, no longer needs the garment, or decides not to collect it.

2.3 Returns of Custom-Made Garments

We do not accept returns simply because a customer changes their mind about a confirmed custom design, fabric selection, or style after production has officially started. This restriction does not remove any rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable Rwandan law.

2.4 Advance Payments

A minimum advance payment of 70% of the agreed order price is required before production can begin. The remaining 30% must be paid in full before or at the time of final pickup or delivery.

The advance payment authorizes the order to proceed once payment is confirmed and Mr. Sewing Fashion sends the official production-start message. Treatment of any cancellation request made before production starts will depend on the work already undertaken, materials committed, the terms confirmed for that order, and applicable law. A refund before production starts is not automatic.

3. Requests Made Before Production Starts

If you need to cancel or change an order, contact us as soon as possible and before receiving the official production-start confirmation.

We will review the order's status and confirm what options are available. If work has not begun, the agreed materials have not been committed, and no other applicable costs have been incurred, we may be able to offer a cancellation or another suitable resolution.

Any approved refund or deduction will be communicated to the customer before the request is finalized. Customers should not assume that a deposit is automatically refundable merely because the official production-start message has not yet been received.

4. Fit Concerns, Adjustments & Quality Assurance

4.1 Our Commitment to a Good Fit

We aim to produce garments that reflect the customer's agreed design and measurement specifications. If you discover a genuine fitting concern or manufacturing defect, contact us promptly so that we can review the issue and determine an appropriate next step.

4.2 The 72-Hour Reporting Window

Fitting concerns and suspected manufacturing defects should be reported within 72 hours (three days) of receiving or collecting the garment.

To help us assess your request, provide your name, order details, a description of the concern, and clear photographs where appropriate. We may ask to inspect the garment or arrange an in-person fitting at our workshop.

4.3 Eligibility for Free Adjustments

Where a reported fitting issue or manufacturing defect qualifies under this policy, we will provide appropriate adjustments without an additional alteration charge, provided that:

  • The concern is reported within the 72-hour window.
  • The garment has not been worn in a way that affects the reported issue.
  • The garment has not been altered by another tailor or third party.
  • The garment has not been damaged, misused, or changed after collection.
  • The concern relates to the agreed order specifications or a manufacturing defect that can reasonably be assessed by our team.

4.4 Assessment and Resolution

We may request that the customer bring the garment to our workshop for assessment. If an eligible issue is confirmed, we will discuss the required adjustment and coordinate the next steps with the customer.

An adjustment is the normal first resolution for an eligible fitting concern or correctable defect. It does not mean that every complaint automatically qualifies for a cash refund or replacement. If an issue cannot reasonably be resolved through adjustment, we will review the circumstances and discuss an appropriate remedy, subject to applicable law.

4.5 Requests Made After 72 Hours

Requests reported after the 72-hour window may be subject to our standard alteration fees. We will explain any applicable charges before carrying out paid work. This reporting window does not remove any rights that applicable law may provide to a customer.

5. Situations That May Not Qualify for Free Adjustments

The free-adjustment procedure is intended to address eligible fitting concerns and manufacturing defects. The following situations may require paid alterations or may not qualify for a free adjustment:

  • Customer-provided measurements: inaccuracies resulting from measurements supplied by the customer, including measurements taken remotely without professional assistance.
  • Changes in preference: requests to change an approved style, design, fabric, or fit preference after production has started.
  • Third-party alterations: changes made by another tailor or service provider after collection.
  • Damage after collection: damage, misuse, or changes caused after the garment has been received by the customer.
  • Late requests: adjustment requests reported after the 72-hour reporting window may incur standard alteration fees.

Each concern will be considered according to the facts. These examples do not automatically determine the outcome where a customer's rights under applicable law require a different result.

6. Customer Responsibilities

To help us deliver the garment agreed upon, customers are responsible for:

  • Providing accurate measurements and contact details.
  • Carefully reviewing the agreed design, fabric, and order specifications before production starts.
  • Communicating requested changes as early as possible.
  • Inspecting the garment promptly after collection or delivery.
  • Reporting fitting concerns or suspected defects within the stated 72-hour window.
  • Keeping the garment in its received condition while an adjustment request is being assessed.

We recommend professional in-person measurement at our workshop whenever possible. Where a customer chooses to provide their own measurements remotely, we cannot take responsibility for fitting errors caused by inaccurate measurements supplied by the customer.

7. Approved Designs, Fabric & Order Changes

Customers should confirm the design, fabric, measurements, and other order details before production begins. Once production has officially started, requested changes may not be possible or may require additional charges and production time.

Slight variations in fabric colour, texture, or appearance may occur due to lighting, screens, fabric characteristics, and the nature of handmade tailoring. Such variations should be assessed in context and do not automatically establish a manufacturing defect.

If you believe that a garment materially differs from the specifications confirmed for your order, contact us within the stated reporting window so that we can review the matter.

8. Collection, Delivery & Uncollected Orders

Customers will be notified when a completed garment is ready for collection or delivery. We ask customers to inspect their garments promptly and contact us if they identify a fitting concern or suspected defect.

Completed garments are held free of charge for seven calendar days from the date the customer receives official notification that the order is ready.

Starting on the eighth calendar day, the Terms & Conditions provide for a daily storage fee equivalent to 10% of the total product cost for each day the garment remains uncollected. The stated policy also provides for an abandonment threshold when accumulated storage fees reach 50% of the product cost.

For full details, including the stated storage schedule and collection arrangements, read our Terms & Conditions .

Storage and collection rules are separate from the process for reporting a fitting concern or manufacturing defect. Customers should contact us promptly if a concern arises.

9. How to Submit a Refund or Adjustment Request

All refund, cancellation, and adjustment requests should be communicated directly to Mr. Sewing Fashion through our official phone or WhatsApp contact.

  1. Contact our team. Explain whether your request concerns cancellation, a fitting issue, a suspected manufacturing defect, or another order-related matter.
  2. Identify your order. Provide your full name, order details, and the date you paid for or received the garment.
  3. Describe the concern. Explain the problem clearly and include photographs where they may help us understand the issue.
  4. Cooperate with the assessment. If necessary, arrange to bring the garment to our workshop for an in-person review or fitting.
  5. Review the proposed resolution. We will communicate the outcome of our assessment and explain any applicable alteration charges or available next steps.

Please retain any order confirmation and payment confirmation that may help us review your request. We aim to handle customer concerns professionally and communicate our decisions clearly.

10. Approved Refunds & Resolution

Where a refund is approved following an assessment, the applicable amount and refund arrangement will be communicated to the customer. The method and timing will be confirmed directly with the customer.

This policy does not promise a refund where the request does not qualify under the agreed order terms. However, nothing in this policy is intended to exclude or restrict a remedy that a customer is entitled to under applicable Rwandan law.

Customers should contact us directly if they have questions about the status of a submitted request.

11. Applicable Law & Customer Rights

This policy should be read together with the Terms & Conditions applicable to the customer's order. Matters not specifically addressed in this policy will be assessed according to the agreed order details and applicable law in Rwanda.

Nothing in this policy is intended to waive, exclude, or limit any mandatory consumer protection right or legal remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded.

If a customer disagrees with the outcome of a request, they are encouraged to contact us first so that we can review the concern and seek a fair resolution.
