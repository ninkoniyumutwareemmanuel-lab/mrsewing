1. Purpose and scope

This policy applies to orders placed with Mr. Sewing, operating under NINKONIYUMUTWARE NEXUS LTD, for bespoke garments, tailoring services, consultations, measurements, fittings and related services.

Cancellation and refund requests are assessed in light of the order stage, work completed, materials committed, the agreed quotation and applicable law.

2. Custom-made orders

Garments are made according to the customer's agreed design, fabric selection and measurements. Before confirming an order, please review the design, fabric, measurements, price, production timeline, delivery or collection arrangements, and any additional service charges.

3. Cancellation before production

If you wish to cancel before production officially starts, contact us as soon as possible and provide your name, order details and reason for cancellation. We will review the order status and any costs already incurred or commitments made. Any refund, deduction or other resolution will be assessed individually and communicated to you; a refund is not automatic solely because production has not started.

4. Cancellation after production begins

Once production starts, fabric may have been cut, materials purchased and tailoring work performed specifically for your order. If you request cancellation, Mr. Sewing will review work completed, materials committed, payments received, the order agreement and applicable law before determining what resolution may be available.

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove a refund or other remedy that must be provided under applicable law.

5. Deposits and payments

  • A minimum 70% advance payment is required before production begins unless a different arrangement is agreed in writing.
  • The production timeline officially starts when you receive the message: “Your order is officially started.”
  • The remaining balance is due before or at final collection or delivery, as stated in the order confirmation.
  • The treatment of a deposit following cancellation depends on the order stage, work completed, costs incurred, the written agreement and applicable law.

If a refund is approved, we will explain the amount and the process for returning the payment.

6. Design changes and adjustments

Changes to design, fabric, colour or measurements after confirmation may affect the price and production timeline. We will communicate additional costs or delays before proceeding with a material change where reasonably possible. Alterations requested because a customer's preferences have changed may incur an additional fee.

7. Fit, defects and order concerns

If you believe a garment has a fitting issue or defect, contact us within 72 hours of receiving or collecting it. Describe the concern and, where useful, provide photographs and your order details.

We will review the concern and determine whether an adjustment or another reasonable solution is appropriate. A free adjustment may be offered where the issue relates to our work and the garment has not been worn, damaged or altered by another person. Later requests, or issues arising from wear, damage, third-party alterations or inaccurate customer-supplied measurements, may incur alteration fees. Applicable legal rights remain unaffected.

8. Matters that may not qualify for a refund

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, a refund may not be available where a garment matches the agreed specifications but the customer changes their preference; incorrect measurements were supplied by the customer; the garment was worn, damaged, washed or altered by a third party after collection; a requested change was not part of the original order; or a completed garment is not collected under the agreed collection arrangements.

These examples do not exclude remedies required by law, including where a garment does not conform to the agreed order or applicable legal standards.

9. Off-site tailor visits, transport and consultation costs

Subject to availability and prior confirmation, Mr. Sewing may arrange professional tailoring consultations, measurements, fittings or other agreed services at a customer's home, office or another location outside our workshop.

Additional charges may apply, including:

  • Transport costs: costs for the professional tailor travelling to and from the agreed location.
  • Consultation fees: fees for professional consultations, measurements, fittings or related services, where applicable.
  • Other agreed service expenses: reasonable additional expenses directly related to the requested off-site service.

Where applicable, these charges may be added to the order price. We will communicate applicable charges before the visit or service is confirmed. Customers should confirm the location, arrangements and additional fees before the appointment.

Unless expressly agreed otherwise in writing, Mr. Sewing is not responsible for covering transport or consultation expenses arising from a customer-requested off-site visit. These charges are separate from garment production price unless the quotation says otherwise.

10. Collection and storage

Completed garments are held free of charge for seven (7) calendar days from notification that the order is ready for collection or delivery. From day eight, a storage fee of 10% of the total product cost per day may apply under our Terms & Conditions. If accumulated storage fees reach 50% of the product cost, the order may be treated as abandoned under those terms, subject to applicable law and mandatory customer rights.

Please contact us promptly if you cannot collect your garment as arranged. See our Terms & Conditions for the full rules.

11. How refund requests are reviewed

To request a refund or another resolution, provide your name and order/payment reference if available, a clear explanation of the issue, the order date and (if relevant) collection or delivery date, and supporting information such as a quotation, payment record, confirmation or photographs.

We may review the quotation, payment records, agreed specifications, communications and production status, and may request further information. We will communicate the outcome and, if a refund is approved, explain the amount and next steps. Processing time can depend on the information available and the payment method; we will not promise a fixed period unless confirmed for your case.

12. Customer responsibilities

  • Check the quotation, design, fabric, measurements, price and timeline before confirming.
  • Provide accurate contact information and accurate measurements when measuring remotely.
  • Keep order confirmations and payment records until the order is completed.
  • Report fitting or quality concerns promptly and allow a reasonable opportunity for assessment.
  • Confirm off-site appointments and any transport, consultation or service charges before the visit.

13. Dispute resolution and arbitration

If you disagree with a cancellation decision, refund assessment, additional charge or another order-related matter, please contact Mr. Sewing first so we can try to resolve it directly. Provide your order details, explain the issue and share relevant quotations, payment records or communications. We will review the available information and aim to communicate in good faith.

If the matter cannot be resolved directly, the parties may consider mediation or arbitration where mutually agreed or available under applicable law. Any arbitration or formal procedure will follow applicable legal requirements, any valid agreement between the parties and the rules governing that procedure.

The applicable procedure will determine arrangements such as appointment of a mediator or arbitrator, timelines and costs. This policy does not set a fixed arbitration fee. Any applicable fees and their allocation will be addressed under the governing procedure, agreement and law.

If the matter is not resolved through an agreed or legally applicable alternative procedure, either party may use a competent court or another lawful dispute-resolution mechanism. Nothing here prevents a customer from exercising a right or remedy that cannot legally be waived.

14. Applicable law and customer rights

This policy is intended to operate in accordance with the laws of Rwanda. If any part conflicts with a mandatory legal requirement, that requirement will prevail to the extent of the conflict. This policy does not limit any consumer protection right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded or restricted.
