Refund & Cancellation Policy

Mr. Sewing · Bespoke Suits · Kigali, Rwanda

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Every Mr. Sewing garment is made specially for you, from your agreed design, fabric and measurements. This policy explains cancellations, fit concerns, adjustments and refunds. Please read it together with our Terms & Conditions and your order confirmation.

Key points at a glance

  • Because every suit is custom-made, we do not offer cancellations, returns or refunds once your order has officially started.
  • Your order officially starts when you receive our message “Your order is officially started.”
  • Tell us about fit problems or defects within 72 hours of receiving your garment. We then offer free adjustments if the garment is unworn, undamaged and not altered by anyone else.
  • Collect your garment within 7 days to avoid storage fees.
  • Nothing here removes any refund or right that the law gives you.

1. Purpose and scope

This policy applies to orders and services from Mr. Sewing, operating under NINKONIYUMUTWARE NEXUS LTD, including bespoke garments, consultations, measurements, fittings and alterations.

2. Custom-made orders

Your garment is made to your agreed design, fabric selection and measurements. Before you confirm, please check the design, fabric, measurements, price, timeline, delivery or collection arrangements and any extra charges. Once you confirm and pay, you agree that the order is made specially for you.

3. Cancelling before production

If you want to cancel before your order has officially started, contact us as soon as possible with your name, order details and reason. We will check the order status and any costs already spent, and tell you what applies. Any refund or deduction is decided for each order and communicated to you. A refund is not automatic just because production has not started.

4. After production starts

Once your order has officially started, fabric may be cut, materials bought and tailoring work done only for you. For this reason, we do not offer cancellations, returns or refunds after the official start.

Nothing in this policy removes a refund or other remedy that must be provided under the law of Rwanda.

5. Deposits and payments

  • A deposit of at least 70% is required before production begins.
  • Your timeline officially starts when you receive: “Your order is officially started.”
  • The remaining balance is paid before or at final collection or delivery, as stated in your order confirmation.
  • If we agree to a refund, we will tell you the amount and how it will be paid back to you.

6. Changes to your order

Changes to design, fabric, colour or measurements after you confirm can affect the price and time. Where we can, we will tell you about extra costs or delays before going ahead. After the official start, changes may not be possible. Alterations you request only because your preference changed may carry an extra fee.

7. Fit, defects and free adjustments

  • Tell us within 72 hours: if you think your garment has a fitting problem or defect, contact us within 72 hours of receiving or collecting it. Describe the problem and, if useful, send photos and your order details.
  • Free adjustments: where the problem relates to our work, we make free adjustments, provided the garment has not been worn, damaged or altered by anyone else.
  • Later requests: requests after 72 hours, or problems caused by wear, damage, other tailors or wrong customer-supplied measurements, may carry alteration fees.
  • Your legal rights are not affected.

8. If we cannot complete your order

If we are unable to complete your order for a reason on our side, we will tell you promptly and agree a fair solution with you. This may include a refund of amounts you paid for work we did not deliver.

9. Matters that may not qualify

A refund may not be available where, for example:

  • the garment matches the agreed order but you changed your mind;
  • the problem comes from measurements you supplied;
  • the garment was worn, damaged, washed or altered by someone else after collection;
  • you asked for a change that was not part of the original order;
  • a finished garment is not collected as arranged.

These examples do not remove any remedy the law requires, for example where a garment does not match what was agreed.

10. Off-site visits and extra costs

Subject to availability and confirmation, we can arrange a consultation, measurements or fitting at your home, office or another place outside our workshop (a VIP mobile house call). Extra charges may apply:

  • Transport: the cost of our tailor travelling to and from the agreed place.
  • Consultation fees: for consultations, measurements or fittings, where applicable.
  • Other agreed expenses: reasonable costs directly linked to the visit.

We tell you any charges before the visit is confirmed, so please confirm the place, arrangements and fees first. Unless we agree otherwise in writing, these charges are separate from the garment price.

11. Collection and storage

Finished garments are stored free for seven (7) calendar days from our notification that your order is ready. From day eight, a storage fee of 10% of the product cost per day may apply. If the accumulated fees reach 50% of the product cost, the garment may be treated as abandoned under our Terms & Conditions, subject to the law and your mandatory rights. Please contact us promptly if you cannot collect on time.

12. How requests are reviewed

To ask for a refund or another solution, send us your name, your order or payment reference if you have one, a clear explanation, the order date and the collection or delivery date, plus any proof such as the quotation, payment record or photos. We may ask for more information. We then tell you the outcome and, if a refund is approved, the amount and the next steps. We do not promise a fixed processing time unless we confirm one for your case.

To help us help you: check your quotation before confirming, give accurate measurements and contact details, keep your payment records until the order is complete, and report problems promptly.

13. Disputes

If you disagree with a decision about a cancellation, refund or extra charge, please contact us first with your order details so we can try to solve it directly and fairly. If we cannot, we may both agree to mediation or arbitration where the law allows. This policy does not set a fixed arbitration fee; any fees follow the procedure, agreement and law that apply. Otherwise, a competent court in Rwanda may handle the matter. Nothing here stops you from using a right that cannot lawfully be waived.

14. Applicable law

This policy is governed by the laws of the Republic of Rwanda. If any part conflicts with a mandatory legal requirement, that requirement applies. See also our Privacy Policy.
