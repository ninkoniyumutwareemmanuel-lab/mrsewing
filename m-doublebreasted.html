Sartorial Distinction

Double-breasted suits project ultimate power and prestige. Featuring a wide overlap of fabric and parallel rows of buttons, this style creates a strong, V-shaped masculine silhouette.

Signature Details

  • Button Stance: Classic 6-on-2 or 4-on-1 button configurations.
  • Lapels: Broad, commanding peak lapels with structured canvas roll.
  • Pockets: Welt left-chest pocket paired with jetted or flap hip pockets.
  • Structure: Enhanced waist suppression and sharp shoulder definition.
