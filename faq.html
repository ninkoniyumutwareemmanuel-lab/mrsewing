Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our bespoke tailoring, ordering, pricing, and services.

Location & Visiting

Where are you located?
Our main workshop is in Kigali, Nyarugenge, Karama, Norvege, KN 441 St. We also operate at the MIC Building Downtown by booking/appointment only.
Do I need to call before visiting?
Yes, please call +250 787 411 501 before visiting so we can provide exact directions and prepare for your consultation.
What are your working hours?
Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Saturday and Sunday are by booking or call. We are closed on the last Saturday of each month for Umuganda.

Booking & Appointments

How do I book a consultation or a visit?
Use our booking page, or call or message us on +250 787 411 501. Choose the type of booking, your preferred date and time, and tell us what you need.
How can I know if my booking has been received?

After you press the booking button, a “Booking Request Received!” screen appears. That means your request reached us.

We then contact you by WhatsApp or email to confirm your date and time. Your booking is confirmed only after we confirm it with you. If you have not heard from us, call or message +250 787 411 501.

What is a VIP mobile house call?
Our tailor comes to your home, office or hotel in Kigali for your consultation, measurements or fitting. Request it on the booking page. We confirm the address, the time and any transport or consultation fee with you before the visit.

Ordering & Production

Do you have ready-made clothes in stock?
No, every garment is custom-made strictly on order to ensure a precision fit.
How do I place an order?
  1. Press Order on our website, agree to the Terms & Conditions, and chat with us on WhatsApp.
  2. We agree your design, fabric and measurements, and send you a quote.
  3. You pay the 70% deposit.
  4. We send you “Your order is officially started” and production begins.
  5. We tell you when your suit is ready, and you pay the remaining 30% at collection or delivery.
How much is the deposit, and when do I pay the balance?
The deposit is at least 70% of the order value, paid before production begins. The remaining 30% is paid before or at the time of collection or delivery.
When does production officially start?
Production begins after a 70% advance deposit is confirmed and you receive our official "Your order is officially started" notification.
How can I know if my order has been officially started?

You will receive our message: “Your order is officially started.” We send it once your 70% deposit is confirmed. Your timeline starts from that message.

If you have paid your deposit and have not received it, contact us on +250 787 411 501 and we will check.

How long does it take to make a suit?
Standard orders take 1 to 2 weeks. Express orders take 1 to 3 days (requires an additional 35% express rush fee).
How can I make an express order?

Tell us you need an express order when you contact us on WhatsApp, by phone or in person. Express orders depend on workshop availability, so we first confirm whether we can take yours.

We then tell you the 35% express fee on the total order value and your ready date, before you pay. Express orders take 1 to 3 days from the official start.

Can I change my order after it has started?
After the official start, changes may not be possible. If we agree to a change, extra cost and time may apply. Please check your design, fabric and measurements carefully before you confirm.
Can I use my own design or an inspiration photo?
Yes. Bring your own design or inspiration photo, and our tailors will craft it to your vision.
How will I know when my suit is ready, and do you deliver?
We notify you when your suit is ready. You can collect it from our workshop, or we can arrange delivery. Delivery details are confirmed with you in advance.

Policies & Discounts

What is your return policy?
Because items are custom-tailored, we do not offer returns or refunds once production begins. We offer free fitting adjustments within 72 hours of delivery. See our Refund Policy.
Can I cancel my order?
Before your order has officially started, contact us as soon as possible and we will review it. After the official start, we do not offer cancellations, returns or refunds because your suit is made only for you. See our Refund Policy.
What if my suit does not fit properly?
Contact us within 72 hours of receiving your suit. We make free adjustments if the garment has not been worn, damaged or altered by anyone else.
What happens if I do not collect my suit on time?
Finished suits are stored free for 7 days from our “ready” notice. From day 8, a storage fee of 10% of the product cost per day applies. At 50% (day 12) the garment is considered abandoned. If you will be late, contact us before day 8. See our Terms & Conditions.
Will you post my photos, and how do I remove them?
We may use photos of our garments, and of customers wearing them, on our social media, website and other platforms, including for advertising. You can ask us at any time to remove your picture, and we will remove it within one week. Contact us on +250 787 411 501.
Do you offer discounts?
Yes! Enjoy 5% off for ordering 2+ items or for returning customers, and 10% off when you refer a friend who makes a purchase.
